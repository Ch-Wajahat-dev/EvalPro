About EvalPro

A web-based project evaluation and management system built to make evaluation transparent, efficient, and fair.

Our Mission

EvalPro was created to modernize the project evaluation process by replacing slow, paper-based workflows with a transparent, real-time digital platform.

Users can submit project proposals, track evaluation progress through every stage, and receive structured feedback — all in one place. Supervisors get a powerful dashboard to manage projects, set evaluation rubrics, and provide scores efficiently.

Our goal is to make project management fair, transparent, and accessible for everyone involved.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide how EvalPro is built and operated.

🔎

Transparency

All evaluation scores and rubrics are visible to students. No hidden grading — every decision is documented and traceable.

⚖️

Fairness

Standardized rubric-based scoring ensures every project is evaluated on equal footing, regardless of who submitted it.

🚀

Efficiency

Streamlined workflows replace paper forms. Supervisors manage dozens of proposals in minutes through one clean dashboard.

🔒

Security

JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and role-based access control protect every account and submission.

📱

Accessibility

Fully responsive design means EvalPro works on any device — desktop, tablet, or smartphone — for every user.

📚

Academic Integrity

The platform is built to uphold university standards. Project submissions, approvals, and records are preserved for accountability.
