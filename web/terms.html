2. Description of Service

EvalPro is a web-based academic project evaluation and management system designed to:

  • Allow students to submit and propose academic projects for evaluation.
  • Enable supervisors to review, score, and provide feedback on submitted projects.
  • Provide transparent, rubric-based or standard scoring methods.
  • Maintain a public listing of approved academic projects.
  • Facilitate communication between students and supervisors regarding project status.

The Platform is provided for academic and educational purposes within Gomal University and associated institutions.
