Everything you need to know about submitting, evaluating, and tracking academic projects on EvalPro.

Step by Step

Pick Your Role

Select your role to see the steps relevant to you.

1
👤

Create Your Account

Visit the Sign Up page and register with your full name, email address, and a secure password. Your account is created instantly — no approval needed.

💡 Use your university email address for easy identification by supervisors.
2
📄

Browse or Propose a Project

Go to the Projects page to browse existing approved academic projects. Use the search bar and category filter to find what you're looking for. If you have your own project idea, click Propose New Project to submit a proposal.

💡 Fill in the project name, description, category, and optionally attach a document (PDF/DOC) to strengthen your proposal.
3
⏳

Wait for Supervisor Review

After submitting your proposal, it enters Pending status. A supervisor will review it and either approve or reject it, optionally leaving a note explaining their decision.

💡 Track your proposal status any time by clicking My Proposals on the Projects page.
4
🎉

Get Approved & Go Live

Once your proposal is approved by a supervisor, it receives an official score and is published to the public projects listing. Other students and supervisors can now view your project.

💡 If your proposal is rejected, read the rejection note and feel free to revise and re-submit an improved version.
5
📊

Track Evaluation Progress

If your project is submitted for a formal evaluation by a supervisor, you can view the full evaluation record — scoring method, rubric used, supervisor comments, and final score — through your account.

💡 For formal evaluation appeals, contact your supervisor or department administrator directly through official university channels.
1
🛠

Login to the Supervisor Dashboard

Use your supervisor credentials to log in. You will be automatically redirected to the Supervisor Panel — a dedicated dashboard not accessible to regular students.

💡 Only accounts with the supervisor/admin role can access the Supervisor Panel. Contact the system administrator to upgrade an account.
2
📝

Review Student Proposals

In the Proposals tab, you'll see all student-submitted project proposals with their status. Click on any proposal to review the project name, description, category, and any attached document.

💡 Use the filter tabs (All / Pending / Approved / Rejected) to quickly find proposals that need your attention.
3
✅

Approve or Reject Proposals

For each pending proposal, you can Approve it with an assigned score (0–100), making it live on the platform — or Reject it with an optional rejection note explaining why.

💡 Approved projects are immediately visible in the public Projects listing. Rejected proposals show the note to the student in their My Proposals view.
4
📄

Manage Projects Directly

Use the Projects tab to add new projects directly, edit existing ones, update scores or status, and delete projects that are no longer active. Uploaded images and documents can be replaced at any time.

💡 The Projects tab shows all projects regardless of approval status, giving you full visibility and control.
5
📊

Record Formal Evaluations

In the Evaluations tab, view all formal evaluations submitted through the platform. Create a new evaluation by selecting projects, choosing a scoring method (Standard, Rubric-Based, Peer Review, or Supervisor-Only), adding comments, and submitting a final score.

💡 Evaluations are permanently recorded and linked to the relevant projects. Students can view their evaluation results in their account.
6
🌟

Create Evaluation Rubrics

Use the Rubrics tab to create time-limited grading criteria for upcoming evaluations. Set a rubric name, description, scoring weight, and deadline — students will see live countdown timers on the homepage.

💡 Rubrics give students clear expectations about grading criteria ahead of their evaluation.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about EvalPro.

Can I submit more than one project proposal? ▼
Yes. There is no limit on how many proposals you can submit. Each proposal is reviewed independently by a supervisor. You can track all your proposals under My Proposals on the Projects page.
What happens if my proposal is rejected? ▼
If a supervisor rejects your proposal, you will see a rejection note in your My Proposals view explaining the reason. You are free to revise your project and submit a new proposal based on the feedback provided.
Who can see my submitted project? ▼
Once your proposal is approved, your project (including your name, description, category, and score) is visible on the public Projects listing to all registered users. Supervisors and administrators can always view all projects regardless of status.
How is the score for my project determined? ▼
Scores are assigned by your supervisor during the approval or evaluation process. Supervisors may use one of four methods: Standard scoring, Rubric-Based scoring, Peer Review, or Supervisor-Only scoring. The method used is recorded with the evaluation.
Can I appeal my evaluation score? ▼
EvalPro records evaluations for reference purposes. For formal grade appeals, you must follow official university procedures and contact your supervisor or department administrator directly. EvalPro does not have a built-in appeal process.
How do I become a supervisor/admin on EvalPro? ▼
Supervisor access must be granted by the system administrator. Regular sign-ups create student accounts by default. Contact the system administrator (muhammadhabeel633@gmail.com) to request supervisor privileges for a verified faculty member.
Is my personal data safe on EvalPro? ▼
Yes. EvalPro uses JWT-based authentication and bcrypt password hashing — your password is never stored in plain text. Role-based access control ensures students cannot access supervisor-only areas. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
